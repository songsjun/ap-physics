## AP Physics 1 Anti-Patterns to AVOID
- Never use μ_k (kinetic friction) for "initial" or "starting" motion — that needs μ_s
- Never ask for instantaneous quantities using only average formulas unless defined
- Never require memorized constants beyond g = 10 m/s²
- Avoid "trick questions" based on units alone; difficulty must come from physics reasoning
- Avoid scenarios that require calculus to derive (e.g., variable forces without work-energy)
